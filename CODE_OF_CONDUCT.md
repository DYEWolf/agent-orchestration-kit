# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Give and accept technical feedback
in good faith. Focus criticism on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized attention,
doxxing, threats, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/DYEWolf/orca-kit/security/advisories/new).
Do not include secrets that are unrelated to the report. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and
may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is inappropriate.

This compact policy is based on the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
